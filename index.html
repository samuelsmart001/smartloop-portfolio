FAQ

Common questions

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the tools and steps involved. Send me your workflow on WhatsApp and I'll reply with a clear scope and fixed price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

A single automation usually takes a few days. A full agent or app with several integrations usually takes one to three weeks. You'll get a timeline with the scope.

What do I need to provide?

A description of how the work is done today, and access to the tools involved. You keep ownership of every account; I work inside them.

What's the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot answers questions. An agent takes action: it processes a lead, updates your CRM, triggers a workflow, or drafts a reply and sends it to you for approval.

Can you fix something another developer built?

Yes. I review the existing project, find where it fails, and fix it without rebuilding what already works.

Do you offer support after delivery?

Yes. Every project includes documentation and a walkthrough, and I can stay on for ongoing fixes and improvements.
